DISCIPLINE 01

Wire-EDM Micro-Machining

Submerged electrical discharge wire eroding carves grade 5 titanium and phosphor bronze bridges with sub-micron 0.005mm geometric repeatability.

Substrate: Ti-6Al-4V Aerospace Grade
DISCIPLINE 02

Hand Anglage & Black Polish

Internal angles are hand-chamfered with boxwood pegs and diamond lapping paste to produce mirror-black specular light reflection at 45° bevels.

Finishing: Côtes de Genève & Perlage
DISCIPLINE 03

Optical Vapor Nanodeposition

Wing membranes are placed in high-vacuum physical vapor chambers to deposit alternating 12-nanometer titanium oxide layers, yielding non-fading structural iridescence.

Optics: 100% Pigment-Free Nanostructures
DISCIPLINE 04

Synthetic Corundum Setting

High-purity synthetic ruby and sapphire bearings are friction-fitted into bridges to reduce axle wear and achieve friction coefficients below 0.04.

Endstones: Cap jewels with Incabloc shock
DISCIPLINE 05

6-Position Dynamic Regulation

Balance wheels are poised using gold micro-screws and tested continuously in 6 physical orientations across a 14-day timing cycle before chassis installation.

Cadence: 21,600 to 36,000 VPH
DISCIPLINE 06

24k Damascene & Hand Filigree

Master engravers hand-cut dovetail micro-grooves into hardened steel carapaces and hammer 99.99% pure 24k gold ribbon under binocular stereomicroscopes.

Gold Inlay: 24-Karat Certified Bullion

Museum Presentation & Conservation

Curated Vitrine Architecture

Every masterwork is delivered in a hermetically sealed, museum-certified quartz cloche engineered to preserve micro-mechanics in pristine condition across generations.

Opti-Clear Hand-Blown Quartz Cloche

99.8% light transmission with dual-sided anti-reflective nanocoating and UV400 museum filter that shields precious gold filigree and thin-film wing foils from photo-degradation.

Hermetic Nitrogen Base Seal (42% RH)

Micro-climate baseline regulator featuring integrated passive zeolite desiccant cells, keeping internal humidity locked at an optimal 42% to completely prevent metal oxidation.

Magnetic Non-Contact Induction Winder

Concealed within the solid smoked basalt pedestal, an electromagnetic pulse driver gently oscillates the automaton's weighted rotor without physical contact, keeping the piece perpetually charged.

2700K Optical Fiber Perimeter Lighting

Warm high-CRI (98+) fiber-optic illuminators radiate cold light from the plinth bezel, revealing depth and microscopic beveling with zero thermal heat emission.
